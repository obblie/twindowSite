FAQ

Common questions

Is it screen mirroring?

Not full-screen mirroring. Twindow is selective duplication of specific app windows.

Does it record my screen?

No. Twindow does not record your screen.

Does it capture keystrokes?

No. Twindows are view-only and do not pass input through.

Will it work with Zoom?

Twindow helps you prepare what to share on a second monitor, but it is not a Zoom plugin.
